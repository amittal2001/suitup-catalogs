How it works

1

Tell us about you

Pick your style preferences and (optionally) add a full-body photo. The photo is processed on your phone and never uploaded anywhere.

2

AI scores every outfit

A neural network trained on tens of thousands of real looks scores how well each combination of items suits you — not just whether the items match each other.

3

Shop or build

Get ranked, complete outfits from partner store catalogs, from your own wardrobe, or around a single item you love. Tap through to the retailer to buy.

FAQ

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs entirely on your phone (via ONNX Runtime). Your photos are stored only on your device and are never transmitted. See the privacy policy.

When is the app available?

SuitUp is in final testing and launching first on Android via Google Play, with iOS to follow. Email us to join the beta.

How does SuitUp make money?

The app is free. When you buy an item through a link in the app or on this site, the retailer may pay us a small commission through affiliate networks. This never affects the price you pay, and it never affects the AI's scoring — outfits are ranked purely by compatibility. See our affiliate disclosure.

Which regions are supported?

v1 supports catalogs for the United States, Europe, and Israel, in English. More regions are planned.

Affiliate disclosure

SuitUp participates in affiliate marketing programs. Some product links in the SuitUp app and on this website are affiliate links: if you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission from the retailer at no additional cost to you. Product recommendations and outfit scores are generated by our AI model based solely on style compatibility — commission rates play no role in what we recommend or how outfits are ranked.
