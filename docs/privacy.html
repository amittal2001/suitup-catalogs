Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

The short version

SuitUp's AI runs entirely on your phone. Your photos — of yourself and of your clothes — are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers or anyone else's. We don't operate servers that receive user data, we don't require an account, and we don't sell data.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your profile photo (optional) — stored on your device only, used on-device to personalize outfit scores. You can skip it, and you can delete it at any time in the app.
  • Wardrobe photos — photos of clothes you add are stored on your device only.
  • Preferences (style selection, region) — stored on your device only.

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of the above.

What leaves your device

  • Catalog downloads — the app fetches product catalog files (public JSON) from our content host. This is a normal HTTPS download; no personal data is sent with it.
  • Retailer links — when you tap "buy", the app opens the retailer's product page in your browser, typically via an affiliate network link (such as AWIN, Rakuten Advertising, or Sovrn) that tells the retailer the visit came from SuitUp. These services may set cookies in your browser per their own privacy policies. No photo or profile data is ever included in these links.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no email collection in the app.
  • No upload of photos or any derived data (including AI embeddings).
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera / photo library — only to let you add a profile photo or wardrobe items. Used on-device only.

Children

SuitUp is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone (see above — it collects essentially nothing).

Changes

If we ever change how data is handled (for example, adding an optional cloud feature), we will update this policy and note it in the app before the change takes effect.
